## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added

- Worksites that disrupt traffic, from the open data of Paris, the Lyon
  metropolitan area, Toulouse Métropole, Rennes Métropole, Tours Métropole and
  Angers, read twice a day, without account nor key.
- A zone around the house (200 m to 2 km) and one zone per street followed,
  each a device: worksites in progress and upcoming, the nearest (or current)
  one, the next one to start.
- Scene triggers: a worksite announced, started or ended in a zone.
- Scene action: the worksites of your zones in one sentence.
- Dashboard widget: the worksites of your zones, nearest first, then the
  upcoming ones.
- English, French, German and Spanish.
